# The Assist

An AI study coach for high school athletes. Short, coached study sessions built around practice schedules, NCAA core-course eligibility tracking, and animated tutors designed with Black and diaspora students at the center.

**Live site:** https://jclaybon1.github.io/the-assist/

## About

Built by [Joshua-James Claybon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jclaybon/): former ODP invitee, two-time Tennessee All-State Jazz first chair, WashU Computer Engineering, Rice MS in AI.

The mission: combine technology, music, and the arts to push students from overlooked places toward academic success.

## Status

This is the landing page for an early-stage project. Prices are planned, the stats shown are examples, and the pilot signup form is not connected yet.
